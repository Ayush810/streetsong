Streetsong

About Streetsong

Streetsong plays free music from independent artists. Every track comes from Audius, an open music platform where artists upload their own work and choose to let anyone stream it. Streetsong doesn't host any music itself. Artists own their tracks, and you can find and support them on Audius.

Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

  • No account, no tracking. Streetsong has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising cookies.
  • Your favourites and playlists stay on your device. They're saved in your browser's local storage and never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Local files stay local. Audio files you add from your computer are played in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Who else sees your requests. When you browse, search or play, your browser talks directly to:
    • Audius (api.audius.co and its content nodes): search terms, track requests and your IP address, under the Audius privacy policy.
    • Our web host: standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security and reliability.
